Scrum master is a specific role for the project scrum to ensure that scrum rules are being protected.

(C)
Sprint Planning: Initiates the cycle. Team decide the plans for the sprint.
Daily Scrum: Daily sync of team members, short meeting. Team members share their progress, as well as bugs or barriers.
Sprint Retrospective: To finish the cycle. The team discuss the performance, and also what to do in next sprint.
